Suggested Patient Messages

Dummy content for the future motivation library.

Treatment

Focus on today's step

You do not have to solve the whole journey today. Let us focus on the next appointment, the next question, and the next small act of care.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For treatment planning visits

Recovery

Rest is part of treatment

Rest is not falling behind. Your body is doing important work, and recovery time is a meaningful part of your care plan.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For post-treatment check-ins

Family Support

Let people help specifically

When someone asks how they can help, try giving one clear task: a ride, a meal, a call, or company during an appointment.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For caregiver conversations

Mindset

Questions are a strength

There is no wrong question about your treatment. Writing questions down before a visit can help you feel more prepared and heard.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For first consultations

Nutrition

Choose gentle consistency

Small, regular meals and enough fluids can be a kinder goal than trying to eat perfectly while your body is healing.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For wellness guidance

Connection

You are still you

Treatment is one part of your life, not the whole of your identity. Make room for music, stories, people, and ordinary moments that feel like you.

Suggested by Dr. Anura Perera · For ongoing support
