Appointment Management

Seamless digital booking system designed to reduce wait times and improve clinic flow for cancer patients.

Overview

Our Appointment Management module streamlines the patient journey by allowing medical staff to efficiently schedule consultations with oncologists and specialists. Instead of traditional booking, patients can easily view their upcoming appointments and track their real-time queue position. By displaying approximate consultation times, the system significantly reduces physical waiting lines and ensures a smoother, less stressful hospital experience.

Key Features

  • Medical Staff Scheduling: Hospital staff manage and assign consultation slots based on doctor availability and patient treatment needs.
  • Real-Time Queue Tracking: Patients can view their approximate consultation time, reducing physical queues and waiting room congestion.
  • Personalized Reminders: Patients can set custom reminders for their scheduled appointments to ensure they never miss a critical session.
  • Centralized Appointment View: A clear, accessible dashboard for patients to view all upcoming consultations, lab reviews, and chemotherapy sessions.

Benefits for Patients

Cancer treatment requires strict adherence to schedules. This system ensures that patients never miss a critical chemotherapy session or follow-up consultation, providing peace of mind and better health outcomes.
