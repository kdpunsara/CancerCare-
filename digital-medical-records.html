Digital Medical Records

Secure, centralized access to patient history, diagnosis reports, and treatment plans for authorized healthcare providers.

Overview

The Digital Medical Records module replaces bulky paper files with a secure, cloud-based repository. It ensures that a patient's complete oncological history—from initial biopsy results to the latest chemotherapy cycle notes—is instantly accessible to authorized doctors, nurses, and pharmacists.

Key Features

  • Centralized Patient History: A single, unified timeline of all diagnoses, surgeries, and treatments.
  • Secure Document Uploads: Nurses and staff can securely upload PDFs of lab results, CT scans, and histopathology reports.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Strict permissions ensure that only the treating doctor and authorized staff can view sensitive medical data.
  • Treatment Tracking: Visual dashboards showing cancer staging, current treatment protocols, and future care plans.

Benefits for Healthcare Providers

Doctors spend less time searching for physical files and more time focusing on patient care. Instant access to historical data allows for faster, more accurate clinical decisions during critical consultations.
